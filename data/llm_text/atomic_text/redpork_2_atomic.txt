1. Cut the pork into evenly sized cubes.
2. Place the pork in a pot of cold water.
3. Skim off the foam.
4. Boil the pork for 3 minutes.
5. Prepare the star anise for stir-frying.
6. Prepare the scallion for stir-frying.
7. Prepare the cinnamon for stir-frying.
8. Prepare the bay leaves for stir-frying.
9. Add a handful of rock sugar to the oil in a wok.
10. Add the pork to the rock sugar when small bubbles appear.
11. Add the star anise to the pork.
12. Add the scallion to the pork.
13. Add the cinnamon to the pork.
14. Add the bay leaves to the pork.
15. Stir-fry the pork and spices briefly until fragrant.
16. Add enough boiling water to the pork and spices all at once.
17. Transfer the pork mixture to a clay pot.
18. Start stewing the pork mixture in the clay pot.
19. Add one cube of red fermented bean curd to the pork mixture.
20. Add some red fermented bean curd brine to the pork mixture.
21. Add 2 spoonfuls of light soy sauce to the pork mixture.
22. Add 2 spoonfuls of dark soy sauce to the pork mixture.
23. Add half a spoonful of oyster sauce to the pork mixture.
24. Add 5 pieces of rock sugar to the pork mixture.
25. Add an appropriate amount of chicken essence to the pork mixture.
26. Add an appropriate amount of thirteen-spice seasoning to the pork mixture.
27. Add an appropriate amount of salt to the pork mixture.
28. Lower the heat to slightly above the lowest setting.
29. Stew the pork mixture for just over 1 hour.
30. Reduce the sauce over high heat.
31. Drizzle some honey over the pork.
32. Adjust the seasoning of the pork.
33. Transfer the pork to a serving plate.
