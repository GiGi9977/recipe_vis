1. Heat the vegetable oil on a gas stove until visible streaks appear.
2. Pour the hot vegetable oil into a large, clean bowl.
3. Sift the cake flour into the hot vegetable oil.
4. Whisk the vegetable oil and flour mixture until evenly combined.
5. Pour the milk into the vegetable oil and flour mixture.
6. Stir the batter until evenly combined.
7. Ensure the bowl for the egg whites is free of oil and water.
8. Crack the eggs gently.
9. Remove each egg yolk with a ceramic spoon.
10. Add the egg yolks to the batter.
11. Gently fold the egg yolks into the batter using Z-shaped strokes until evenly combined.
12. Set the egg yolk batter aside.
13. Add the salt to the egg whites.
14. Add the lemon juice to the egg whites.
15. Beat the egg whites until large bubbles form.
16. Add one-third of the sugar to the egg whites.
17. Beat the egg whites until they turn white.
18. Add another one-third of the sugar to the egg whites.
19. Add the remaining one-third of the sugar to the egg whites.
20. Beat the egg whites at a lower speed until they form a large curved hook when the beaters are lifted.
21. Add one-third of the beaten egg whites to the egg yolk batter.
22. Fold the beaten egg whites into the egg yolk batter until evenly combined.
23. Add the remaining beaten egg whites to the egg yolk batter.
24. Gently fold the remaining beaten egg whites into the batter until evenly combined.
25. Cut the parchment paper to fit the mold.
26. Cut the cardboard to fit the mold.
27. Place the cardboard between the parchment paper and the mold.
28. Pour the cake batter into the mold from a height.
29. Smooth the surface of the cake batter with a scraper.
30. Preheat the oven to 150°C.
31. Bake the cake on the lower-middle rack at 150°C for 60–70 minutes.
32. Gently press the cake after 60 minutes to check whether it feels firm.
33. Bake the cake for another 10 minutes if it still feels soft and makes a rustling sound.
