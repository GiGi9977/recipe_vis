Recipe text（整理后的流程文字）:
1. 虾去壳、去虾线。
2. 加入葱、姜、料酒、生抽、蚝油、盐和黑胡椒，腌制约 20 分钟。
3. 碗中打入 2 个鸡蛋，加入 3 勺淀粉、盐和黑胡椒，搅拌均匀制成面糊。
4. 将腌好的虾裹上面糊。
5. 油烧热后放入虾，炸至微黄。
6. 待油温升高后，将虾复炸至金黄色，捞出。
English process:
peel shrimp → devein shrimp → add scallion, ginger, cooking wine, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, salt, and black pepper → marinate for 20 minutes → combine eggs, starch, salt, and black pepper → mix into batter → coat shrimp with batter → heat oil → deep-fry shrimp until lightly golden → increase oil temperature → deep-fry again until golden → drain → finish
